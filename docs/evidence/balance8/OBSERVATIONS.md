## Retained limits

These interventions do not author a new local-production/export district or long-commute expansion. The bus arm purchases normal infrastructure and fleet; it cannot demonstrate a remote district benefit when no remote district was authored. Tariff protection is a sourcing incentive, not proof of a fully local supply chain. The food-only arm can act only if a food offer actually exists; an unavailable offer is retained, not replaced with a fabricated commitment. Supplier-contracts accepts the first available normal offer. Separate urban and transit owners cover placement, relocation and passenger routing/capacity; those effects cannot be inferred from the legacy commuter budget.

Public-services stops new private licenses and raises public budgets; it preserves inherited private incumbents and commissioned projects. It is a public-emphasis comparison, not a confiscation-based pure public economy. Private and public school coverage use different resident/learner denominators. Compare paid learners, learning completions and household distribution alongside either index. Initial service reports are cleared during matched archive restore; use day-30 onward snapshots. Births and inward arrivals are detected from actual new resident identities; deaths/outward migration include the warmup and should be differenced against initial counts. External commodity flows are cumulative ledger quantities. Regional cash changes are net external financial flows, not gross trade-dollar turnover.

Full annual/monthly samples retain conservation, defaults, household liquidity quantiles, births/deaths/migration, prices, firms and public/private provision. No test result certifies fun, physical Android FPS or a real-world economic claim.

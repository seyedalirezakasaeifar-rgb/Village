# Regional trade implementation checkpoint

Three named regional counterparties have distinct import/export price advantages, travel times and finite shared daily handling capacities that change monthly. Their cash is explicitly the existing conserved external-region account, not three invented cash pools.

Road imports pay supplier/carriage/tariff on dispatch and create town-owned in-transit stock. Wholesalers and retailers can order only with cash and road access. Pending inbound inventory prevents duplicate orders. Goods become usable on arrival. Road exports reserve supplier inventory and pay carriage at dispatch; title, buyer payment and export/COGS recognition occur on arrival. Insufficient buyer liquidity delays settlement; it cannot mint receipts. Routes and accounting continue when zoom LOD hides trucks. River settlement remains the existing collection/title-transfer model and is explicitly distinguished in the UI.

The exchange shows quotes, shipments and limited30-day supplier agreements. The mayor reviews stock, time, freight and approximate inventory margin; firm revenues remain with the supplier. Food/working-stock buffers limit contract dispatch. Cancellation stops future dispatch, not cargo already in transit. Missed windows remain visible; no arbitrary penalty, invented reward or retroactive confiscation. Active agreements are capped and retained history bounded.

Truck sprites correspond to actual consignments and travel along gateway-to-enterprise or enterprise-to-gateway paths, interpolated with simulation time. They can be inspected through the exchange or directly on the map. Gateways currently represent regional-road connections at extrema of the connected local road network; dedicated entrance architecture, world-edge highway rights and richer loading/queuing choreography are next visual work, not already complete.

Current scope limits: household shopping remains retail rather than individual overseas ordering; local sourcing still precedes fallback regional imports, so landed-price competition needs further refinement. Service exports retain existing finite service pools. Contract history has no separate reputation multiplier yet. The first-contract tutorial, dedicated trade dashboard/action hierarchy, stronger district/campaign consequences and private services remain subsequent integrated work. Do not describe this checkpoint as the completed8.0 expansion.

Targeted tests cover paid imports and town-owned exports in transit, exact-once arrival, conserved cash/commodities, unfunded buyer delay, actual agreement controls,90-day economy progression, valid round trip and atomic rejection of malformed freight. Actual exchange pixels were inspected. The frozen phase assembly passed core release gates and all15 three-year scenario/seed runs. Earlier runs interrupted by source edits are not final evidence.

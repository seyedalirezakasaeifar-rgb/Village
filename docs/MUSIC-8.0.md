# An original chamber score for TownLedger

Three fully rendered original pieces: Morning Ledger (3:22), River and Road (2:52), Lamplit Windows (3:44). About ten minutes, with plucked strings, sustained harmonic voicings and a breathing wind melody. Melodies,64-bar arrangements and additive synthesis are authored in tools/compose_music.py; no external samples or borrowed recordings. Source and assets include CC0 dedication. Offline rendering requires NumPy and FFmpeg; the app does not synthesize instruments at runtime.

First genuine click unlocks playback after founding/Continue. Options has separate music toggle, volume and next piece; existing ambience/effects mute does not mute music. Preferences persist independently of saves. Background/native suspension pauses all decks. Resume keeps only the current piece. Equal-power four-second transitions blend at most two referenced media decks; normal clicking does not reset the transition. Known score durations determine transitions because streamed Vorbis metadata can temporarily underestimate duration in some HTTP/WebView implementations. Playback failures are surfaced in Options with a tap-to-retry path.

Actual Chromium HTML media playback, browser decoding of all three Vorbis files, nonzero stereo signal, persisted volume, independent effects mute, host interruption/resume and transition cleanup passed. The near-end transition branch is exercised with a test-only clock property; a complete ten-minute auditory listening review and physical Android audio/decoder certification remain pending. These tests demonstrate routing and signal, not that a listener will enjoy the compositions. No subjective listening claim is made.

Each decoded piece is streamed by HTML media rather than retained as a WebAudio buffer. The test decoder intentionally loads one full piece at a time to inspect PCM; the app does not do that. Runtime heap/decoder peak measurement remains part of integrated final acceptance. The three compressed assets total about3.8MiB.

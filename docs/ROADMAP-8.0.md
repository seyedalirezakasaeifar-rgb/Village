# 8.0 — A city worth returning to

Deadline: November1 07:00 America/Vancouver, resolved with runtime zoneinfo; final acceptance starts at least two hours earlier. Preserve7.1.1 as the recovery baseline. No claim that existing ambient WAVs are music: there is currently no soundtrack.

## Player journey

First10minutes: choose a town identity and nearby trading partner, follow a paid delivery from gateway to a shop, hear the town theme, solve one visible shortage, and earn a choice of two development opportunities. Explain money, goods and access through that delivery rather than a page of tax sliders.

First hour: specialize one district, negotiate a small recurring supply contract, connect the useful parcel, choose municipal capacity or a fee-charging private provider, survive a manageable supply disruption, and reinvest the proceeds. Rewards should open choices rather than make the rich town automatically richer.

Three hours and beyond: establish two complementary neighbourhoods and alternative supply routes, balance public access against private fees, finance a major civic/trade project, and navigate changing outside demand. Chapter objectives connect these actions and offer multiple solutions. Recovery remains possible after a missed contract or overinvestment; no arbitrary irreversible punishment.

## Connected implementation pillars

1. Named outside markets with distinct offers, demand, distances and accessible price histories; prices are explainable and budget bounded.
2. Physical regional consignments: dispatch/payment/ownership/arrival recorded once, gateway truck routes visible and inspectable, congestion/capacity and delay matter.
3. Recurring trade agreements with minimum lots, delivery windows and explicit cancellation/default terms; spot trade remains a recovery path.
4. Logistics depots and inventory buffers: investment buys reliability and handling capacity; storage and credit have real costs.
5. Parcel prospectuses showing resource/access advantages, required infrastructure, alternative uses and district opportunity costs before purchase.
6. District development programmes: connect, service and specialize a neighbourhood, then unlock a consequential project rather than merely more empty tiles.
7. Entrepreneur-owned schools and fire brigades with actual financing, recruitment, service capacity, receipts, expenses and closure/recovery.
8. Public-private service choices: tuition/membership affordability, targeted municipal purchasing, access and quality reports; private success must not silently grant universal free coverage.
9. Zoom-aware presentation budgets: no invisible pedestrian/car work at distant zoom, aggregate economy unchanged; informative regional routes replace microscopic actors.
10. Original coherent visual identity: freight gateways, market-specific cargo, neighbourhood landmarks, active service fronts, construction and arrival animation, consistent silhouettes/materials/lighting.
11. Original multi-part soundtrack, independent music/effects controls, gesture unlock, lifecycle pause and smooth theme transitions. Long composed phrases and variation, not a short oscillator loop.
12. A connected civic campaign with several chapter paths, contracts, district milestones and investment projects; readable causal feedback, nonspam events and recovery choices.
13. A useful city ledger: near-term bottlenecks, expected policy/service lags, shipment status and contract commitments, with map links.
14. Guided first delivery and optional contextual hints; touch-accessible controls and consistent navigation, with a compact report of what changed while time advanced.

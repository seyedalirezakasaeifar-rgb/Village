# Settings and accessibility

In **Settings → Music & atmosphere**, turn music on or off, adjust music volume, and choose **Follow the town**, **Quieter selections**, or **Livelier selections**. Music and ambience/sound effects have separate controls. Music begins after your first tap; **Next piece** skips to another selection. Your choices persist after reload. Backgrounding or suspending the app pauses audio; returning resumes according to your mute settings. If playback is interrupted, use **Retry music**.

In **Display & accessibility**, **Reduced motion** stops decorative movement and traffic animation. **Larger text** enlarges menu labels and reading text. The **Performance / Balanced / Rich detail** setting, frame-rate limit, visible traffic, atmospheric effects, and garden detail adjust visual load. These choices persist after reload.

Checked in Chromium at 360×640: music and effects were paused when muted; mute, reduced motion, larger text, low graphics detail, traffic, effects, and decoration settings persisted after reload. Reduced motion held the presentation clock steady while simulation ran. Browser background/return and native host suspend/resume kept mute in effect. Keyboard Tab moved from the music toggle to **Next piece**, with visible focus. Settings controls fit within the viewport horizontally.

These checks cover a host browser and a narrow viewport; they do not certify assistive technology or physical Android devices.

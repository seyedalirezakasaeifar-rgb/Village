# Music and sound

Music starts after your first tap. In **Settings → Music & atmosphere**, you can turn music on or off, set its volume, and choose **Follow the town**, **Quieter selections**, or **Livelier selections**. Music and sound effects have separate controls. Use **Next piece** to skip; the selector avoids the six most recently started pieces. If playback is interrupted, tap **Retry music**. Your music choices persist between sessions.

When the app goes into the background or is suspended, playback pauses. Returning to the town resumes the current piece. No sound starts on a fresh launch until you interact with the game.

The soundtrack contains ten original pieces, about 47 minutes in total. Their compositions, arrangements, recordings, and generation code are dedicated under CC0; see [the soundtrack license](../../assets/music/LICENSE.txt). Music and sound effects may be muted independently.

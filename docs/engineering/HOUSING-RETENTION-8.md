# A household at the council door

Once a month, the council may receive a non-interrupting letter when one of the eight lowest-cash occupied households has a real emigration assessment: a persistent local budget deficit, under ninety days of spendable essentials, financial distress, and a better outside reference. The case names the household and reports its live budget and outside comparison. It is not generated from town-wide hardship alone.

The mayor can transfer up to fourteen days of the assessed local shortfall (capped at $3,000) from municipal reserves to the household, or adopt the existing `Learning within reach` civic project for that family when a working-age adult is present. The first choice offers short-term runway without promising retention or a job. The second makes no immediate transfer, but requires actual funded school access and a completed adult training term. Existing skills work is not overwritten; if one is active, the alternate option preserves the reserve. Defer keeps the letter available.

The assessment and quoted amount are recomputed immediately before either choice. An improved or otherwise changed case is rejected without charge. Household transfer uses the normal council paid-choice settlement path, so reserves fall by the displayed amount, the named household receives it, and total money is conserved. A paid bridge is recorded in the existing council archive; its unresolved receipt is retained through ordinary archive trimming, and another bridge remains unavailable until the underlying departure risk clears. If the family still meets the actual emigration assessment after receiving cash, the existing outward-migration system can still let them leave. Council inbox, archive and civic-project state use existing save fields and save hooks.

`tests/test_housing_retention_dilemma.py` exercises actual Agenda and decision controls, save/load and defer, the training-project path, an exact cash transfer and total-money conservation, repeated callback and monthly-offer guards, atomic insufficient funds, a household departing while the quote is open, an improved budget clearing the bridge gate, and a later risk returning. It also forces the existing outward-migration random draw after the bridge to confirm the family can still leave when its risk assessment remains eligible. `node --check assets/game.js` passes.

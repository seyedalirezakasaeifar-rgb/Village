# Targeted heap and draw-tail diagnosis

The remote `codex/8-city-continuity` tip is verified at full commit `2fc451f5b85b19cbba6166be418fd44e0a4554ec`. Investigation used an archived immutable copy of source commit `d4607eefbadee15a8347568f4e5e8ade7a1c322c`, with game bundle SHA-256 `6260809a99d39d3f3c8131bcd9e85343695617d5629950409cca1ba7ef2aa1aa`.

To isolate the minute-20–25 heap step, I repeated the same 3,091 transition-index interval (12,376–15,467) three times on the paused 2,000-resident, 21-deed, three-route fixture. It alternated overlays, camera positions/zoom, day/night, quality and reduced motion, and performed six full cache resets. Heap snapshots were forced through GC after the fixture and each repeat. The timing arrays were drained before each snapshot to avoid retaining profiler data.

Post-GC JS heap was 21.7 MB at baseline and after all three repeats. Retained-node counts rose from 246,738 to 256,751 after the first repeat, then settled at 253,069 and 253,252 on repeats two and three. External string backing data grew once from 2.07 MB to 3.03 MB by the second repeat, then remained flat; the one large state-checkpoint string is test instrumentation. Atlas use settled at 2.27 MB, night emission at 0.61 MB, and DOM node count remained 406. No progressive retained-object or cache growth reproduced. This repeated interval did not reproduce the 17.1→33.1 MB step from the 30-minute host soak; it does not identify the source of that one-time heap step.

Draw timing separated view-key rebuilds from unchanged-key redraws. Across three identical passes, rebuild draw p50 was 35.4–35.6 ms, p95 189.5–190.4 ms, and p99 218.9–224.6 ms (max 277–295 ms). Warm-cache redraw p50 was 1.2 ms, p95 3.5 ms, and p99 8.7–10.7 ms (max 47–62 ms). Two explicit full-cache resets per pass measured 47–75 ms median and 79–129 ms maximum; with only two observations per pass, those reset percentiles are not statistically meaningful. Repeated unchanged-camera draws hit the warm path; no redundant rebuild loop was found. These are host Chromium software-render measurements, not Android WebView frame-rate estimates.

One transient frame comparison changed 62 pixels across a small 28×3 water-highlight patch during the first profiler attempt. Game and RNG state matched. The next six resets in corrected measurement runs matched exact pixels. The raw frames and contrast-enhanced crop are preserved in [overlay-heap-8](../previews/overlay-heap-8/), and the full snapshot/category and timing summary is [replay.json](../previews/overlay-heap-8/replay.json). Given the isolated mismatch and exact subsequent matches, I made no renderer source change.

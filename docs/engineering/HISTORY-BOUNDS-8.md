# Completed-record retention and forty-year save growth

Read-only production audit of bb02bbc, assembled SHA-256
8c7d3216d05355c6b15467c357bdc71f34e14d3714204f7a43c5e4cb8b03b26c.
Executable test: tests/test_history_bounds.py. No scheduling/economic production
code changes, generated whole archives or user saves are included.

Controlled pressure uses actual paid authorization/cancellation and existing
regional capital transferred into the treasury, never invented funds:

- 1,000 calendar preparations/cancellations retain 16 event rows. A pre-existing
  future event remains byte-identical while finished/cancelled rows are retired.
  Archive characters stabilize at 164,739 over cycles 300–900 (164,742 at cycle
  1,000 as serial digits grow), rather than one record per completed gathering.
- A genuinely assessed/acquired/funded ground-work obligation remains identical
  throughout calendar pressure; no pruning erases the paid municipal reservation.
- 1,000 two-stage procurement groups retain at most 24 contract rows. A cancelled
  group with still-owned paid cargo remains present; all 13 live consignments and
  complete group references survive byte-identically. Each cycle validates IDs.
- 10,000 migration diagnostic reviews retain 80 recent entries. That recent list
  is transient session data, not an ever-growing archive census.

One unassisted seed-42 simulation advances forty 360-day years in twenty two-year
blocks, measuring normal serialize/import behavior and checking cash/goods/town
invariants. Every sampled archive loads. Exact thirty-day late continuation passes.

Across samples, maximum serialization was 12.6ms and import 27.7ms on this host.
Final cash drift was -$0.000090; commodity invariants remained within 0.001 units.
The archive grows with actual town/person/estate history and reaches observation
retention, not indefinitely with completed event rows. These host timings are not
Android performance certification and this is not another timed hour soak.

Renewal records are a persistent physical-ground/title provenance ledger, not a
simple expiring event feed. They are not pruned blindly: assessed/acquired/
remediating/ready records reserve actual land; reused records preserve origin IDs.
The importer currently caps that ledger at 3,000 entries, households/firms at 3,000
and people at 8,000. Those are finite archive acceptance limits, not proof that
unlimited redevelopment or immortal record growth is supported. The natural
forty-year arm did not create renewal sites; pressure specifically proves retention
of one funded outstanding site, not a 3,000-site renewal saturation result.

No confirmed completed-history leak warranted a production pruning change. Future
provenance compaction must first establish that a retired site has no remaining
building-origin, reserved land, project, cargo or financial references. Historical
people/estate/title records cannot be deleted merely to flatten a size chart.

Two setup pilots correctly refused a founding-day forecast and an assumed paid
consignment. The final fixture advances real production and waits for actual
contract dispatch before pinning cargo. Those were fixture assumptions, not
reclassified engine defects. Full source-bound measurements are in
`docs/evidence/history-bounds8/result.json`; no whole checkpoints are published.

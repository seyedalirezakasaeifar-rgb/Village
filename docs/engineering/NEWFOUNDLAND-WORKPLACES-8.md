# Original civic and working-building kit

Prototype-only continuation of the composite district/batching candidate. Adds original reusable massing for 17 actual categories: town hall, school, college, courthouse, police, clinic, fire, bank, office, warehouse, factory, mill, sawmill, contractor, fishery, wharf and farm. Symmetrical civic glazing, stone bank pilasters/stringcourses, apparatus/loading doors, lower workshop/barn volumes and restrained roof/eave materials replace their generic house fallback. Waterfront frontage chooses an actual adjoining water direction when available. A closure barrier requires actual firm/provider closure; inactive but open services are not falsely fenced closed. No crews, passengers, sales, crop yields or new buildings are invented.

Only rectangular legal properties are supported by this module; concave titles explicitly decline rather than building through their void. The 68 one-tile category/orientation envelope cases pass, as does explicit concave rejection and source-state invariance. Existing five-category housing/shop kit retains 40 envelope tests. This is generalized regional art, not a copy of any landmark or photo. Farm fields/crops, dock structures, church entities, airport, tall blocks, transit structures and live actors remain separate incomplete work. This is not production acceptance or complete family coverage.

Source-matched actual-control, paired same-town render and camera-scale results live under docs/previews/newfoundland-workplaces-8. Performance remains host SwiftShader only. The fixed local scene still lacks full-world streaming and animated actors; cached static shadows will need a separately updated dynamic shadow layer when actors arrive.

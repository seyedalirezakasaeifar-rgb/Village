# Committed architecture context and original family forms

Prototype-only continuation of ee663227. The snapshot caches actual model district, household, tenure, provider, firm, project and physical-condition fields at the existing revision boundary. It optionally exports `urbanBuildingDescriptor(b)` as `architecture`, consuming the authoritative contract from district candidate b7a6039a799b2ceac38903f85da45933b61d4154. This prototype base predates that API: its absence produces neutral compact timber homes, not a second renderer-side income or district classifier. Integration must supply the district candidate before claiming observed district-specific forms.

The pure family selector maps `buildingCue` to original row, saltbox, suburb and merchant forms. Merchant massing requires an actual multi-tile property; no cue enlarges a lot. Income never controls wear. Raw physical condition alone changes trim/material weathering. New original saltbox/gable roofs and bungalow/biscuit-box proportions extend the five-kind kit; active redevelopment/worksites render fenced foundations instead of finished generic buildings. Project fencing does not imply paid workers or progress.

This is partial geometry coverage, not completion of all 34 categories. Civic, industrial, network, waterfront, public-space and tall/multi-tile specialized forms remain pending as listed in NEWFOUNDLAND-COVERAGE-8. No absent church/community-hall entity is introduced. The shared adapter will need the committed worker projection contract and broader revision invalidation before production promotion.

Verification: pure family tests exercise authoritative cue selection, independent condition, fallback and legal area limits; 40 five-kind/orientation/attachment envelope tests pass. HTTP context projection leaves the archive byte-for-byte unchanged (102 buildings, initial plus ten cached reads 4.2ms host, no errors). Actual-control regression covers inspection, paid road placement/live rebuild, navigation, portrait, save and Canvas fallback. Host measurements are not Android performance. The previous quality benchmark's severe SwiftShader RAF costs remain unresolved; this commit does not claim a performance fix.

Architectural reference matrix was supplied by the research owner and is recorded in the coverage inventory. Private concept pixels and public photo bytes remain unavailable through the attempted supported routes; no photo inspection or exact concept matching is claimed, and no photo is bundled.

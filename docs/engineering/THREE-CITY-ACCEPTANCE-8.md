# Three coherent city journeys on frozen candidate31987fc

## Finding

The combined game supports materially different capital commitments and recoverable real failures. It still does not demonstrate several hours of authored decision variety. A working-river direction completes by day81 (2.2 minutes at1x), while later community directions can remain stalled on an unfinished district even as municipal cash exceeds$2million. Waiting is neither proof of content nor a necessary recovery strategy: a targeted local recovery finishes the district after building its missing actual homes/services.

No production files were changed. Scalable education/health budget controls and migration refinements were pending on this checkpoint. This audit does not characterize their later integration.

## Method and actual time

`tools/audit_three_cities.py` runs three fresh seed42 towns at844×390 through actual buttons, policies, quotes, construction-map taps and normal borrowing controls. Probes read state, survey legal sites, project camera coordinates and advance normal ticks. There are no cash/stock/worker/skill grants, forced provider entry or economic coefficient changes. Council petitions are explicitly deferred through the normal control, not silently accepted by the test step helper. Their optional choices were not exercised and remain in the inbox; this comparison cannot rule out additional event-driven variety.

Each cohort reaches day4365: roughly120 minutes at the nominal1x clock, almost12 simulated years. These are NOT two-hour human play sessions. Completed automated cohorts took50.8 seconds(port),80.2 seconds(transit),53.0 seconds(local), including settlement, navigation and recorded waits. They contain15,14,16 non-petition planned decision attempts and65,58,68 button clicks. Automation does not measure human planning time, reading time or enjoyment. Many later simulated months are deliberately observed without further investment; this establishes outcomes under those policies, not absence of every possible voluntary player action.

Read-only probes and receipts use source SHA-256 recorded in `reports/three-city-audit/summary.json`. Compressed cohort receipts include actions, controls, full checkpoints, closures and the final actual save. Recovery continuations load those saves, use real controls and observe60 additional days. Raw unsuccessful control attempts are retained rather than relabelled as completed gameplay.

## Recovery evidence and corrected audit navigation

* The newly commissioned river wharf closes on day440 with cash0. The earlier certificate remains, and current-role recovery is available. A late real recovery first purchases a three-tile$450 spur, then separately reviews/commissions a replacement wharf. After60 days the new wharf has one attending worker, about$8,530 cash and only about$2 revenue on the sampled day. Physical/crew recovery succeeds; profitability is NOT established. The original town wharf remains open and earns about$68 that day. A new waterfront building does not automatically attract enough trade.
* Transit frequency/suspension attempts in the long cohort initially hit collapsed route disclosures: audit navigation omitted opening them. This is not evidence of a broken control. The recovery script opens the actual disclosure, suspends the route, reviews the$6,200 extra vehicle/up-to$139 operating quote and authorizes frequent service. After60 days it operates with four actual drivers,135 peak places and16 journeys on the final sampled day. Other changes also occur; this is not proof that frequency alone caused the additional trips.
* Local district residents remain0 despite the town having354 residents. The original housing attempt targeted a nearby global survey rather than restricting it to the project tract; it does not prove project housing is impossible. The corrected continuation uses legal sites INSIDE Orchard Downs and purchases four homes($80,000), clinic($8,000), school($6,900) through map controls. It adds eight residents through the existing normal home-construction mechanic, reaches99% care/70% teaching locally, and completes the district on day4395. No grants or forced workers are used. This is a useful actual recovery, not a claim that the long delay was necessary.
* At day365 the public loading-court action has no shared vacant site. The button remains enabled; it toasts an assembly suggestion and supplies no naming dialog. The first port attempt had a harness error clicking the drawer close after the build tool already closed it. That incomplete attempt was discarded; the committed final cohort uses actual map taps. No source change was made between cohorts. Later disclosure/housing survey errors remain visible in raw receipts and are resolved by the bounded actual recovery continuations.

## Pacing and recommended next work

**First10-minute-equivalent interval:** there are strong concrete capital choices. Food buffer versus bakery, square versus handling site, river rights versus transit, and operating services versus private entry all spend real scarce initial resources. However, the river direction's first two stages are awarded on days2/3 from existing town activity before the new district wharf is commissioned on day14. The district certificate follows day51 and the direction day81. This is a short orientation track, not a long campaign.

**First-hour-equivalent interval:** meaningful current issues emerge—new wharf insolvency, changing service access, underused transit, a district without its intended residents—but most require the mayor to invent the next reason to act. Repeated import/agreement/household records do not by themselves provide a new strategic dilemma. A persistent certificate can coexist with a failed new wharf for years; current warnings help, but the chosen city identity has little pressure to recover before proceeding.

**Later interval:** all three accumulate substantial money with modest discretionary intervention. The service-heavy strategies do not demonstrate a globally dominant strategy, but there is little observed sustained municipal financing pressure once mature. Advancing years while opening largely similar reports does not establish hours of engaging decisions. The unfinished districts are missing concrete purchases, not authored content waiting to unfold.

Prioritize these connected refinements over additional menus or counters:

1. **Turn the chosen district into the next actionable operating plan.** On the existing card, name missing local residents/places, identify the actual tract boundary, show two legal nearby housing/service alternatives and the total staged cost. A town-wide population or school figure must not imply the district is served. This makes the observed$94,900 recovery discoverable early, when it competes with real capital, rather than after two million accumulates.
2. **Make waterfront decisions depend on observed business prospects.** Before a second wharf, show existing wharf crews/receipts, recent actual paid river traffic and the new site's access. After closure, offer the existing compensated reuse/alternate charter alongside replacement. Explain the forgone working shore plot. Do not promise cargo, subsidize losses automatically or add an arbitrary identity buff.
3. **Connect passenger service to named destinations and observed underuse.** For this route, no denied demand plus$2.60 fares/$70.53 operations should invite comparing stops, nearby employment/teaching and lower service BEFORE a frequency upgrade. Show actual accessible destinations and the lost public spending alternative on the same existing route card. Preserve affordable public service as a valid choice; low fare recovery is not itself a failure condition.

Small supporting UI correction: replace unavailable square/loading/renewal affordances with specific current reasons and direct legal next actions, while retaining revisitable historic places. More elaborate culture/identity features should wait for these existing place commitments to produce a clearer decision loop.

Human enjoyment remains unverified. The evidence supports real choice diversity and recovery, but engagement is still the strongest acceptance gap.

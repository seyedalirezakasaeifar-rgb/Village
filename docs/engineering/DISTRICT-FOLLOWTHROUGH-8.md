# District follow-through and honest direction provenance

Base stable `4e9248c` (includes scalable service-budget controls). This scoped change edits no economic coefficient, staffing, freight or redevelopment engine.

## Finish the tract that was actually chosen

Active mixed-neighbourhood project cards now expose their own residents/8, attending workplaces/2 and local care/teaching/40% beside an actionable, spatially valid capital envelope. The plan includes missing business sites, required purpose streets, ordinary homes and conditional clinic/school sites. Every proposed footprint stays inside the actual tract, connects to existing legal roads and avoids every companion site and the purpose blueprint's reserved advice. It never grants exclusive reservations. If a legal business-purpose plan cannot fit, the card explains the unavailable footprint and offers street/compensated-site diagnosis rather than selling a misleading plan.

The envelope is not a mandatory shopping list. Homes come first; actual resulting coverage is reassessed before recommending service capital. Existing live enterprises count toward the two-business requirement: missing attendance leads to staff/access inspection, not duplicate enterprise purchases. Existing public/private school/clinic structures are not automatically duplicated when provision is low. Current staffing/provision and the actual scalable teaching/care authorization controls are linked directly. Opening them changes neither authorization nor draft values, and enactment remains the existing explicit action.

Each button reviews one exact footprint and its price. No multi-site autocharge is introduced. House/clinic/school commits revalidate the entire companion plan, current project/town, placement rules and funds before calling `construct`. Business and street reviews delegate to the existing purpose-blueprint purchase path. Street quotes are a separate reviewed prerequisite when needed; enterprise prices are gross and normal regional permit receipts affect net municipal cash. Ordinary new homes retain the existing household-settlement mechanic; reserved replacement homes remain a separate compensated-relocation mechanism.

## Existing activity is not newly earned work

Newly selected directions capture an optional `mayorJourney.baseline` version1: delivered count, fulfilled agreement IDs, completed district certificate signatures and all existing business IDs, plus already-met essentials/services prerequisites. New trade stages require additional successful deliveries or newly fulfilled agreements. District stages require a completion certificate beyond the baseline, including a genuine new charter on an earlier tract. River stages require new staffed enterprises beyond the businesses present when the direction was selected; initial operating docks/fisheries cannot automatically earn new work on days2/3. Stage text makes this requirement explicit and provides inspection of existing waterfront demand/accounts before competing for trade.

Essentials/services already met at selection are explicitly recorded and displayed as EXISTING PREREQUISITE rather than newly achieved work. No artificial counter or longer waiting period was added. The existing final sustained-service requirement remains unchanged. Legacy active directions without a baseline retain their original progression rules and are labelled as legacy on affected role descriptions; completed certificates are never reset or reinterpreted as new baselined work. Optional baseline and completion provenance fields are validated by the existing mayor-journey validator, including bounded unique references and valid district certificate signatures.

# Market gathering or essential-goods handling

The existing gathering review now compares three explicit actions when already-paid imports actually wait in the county-interchange loading/unloading queue: pay gathering preparation; instead authorize essential-import priority with contingent paid extra shifts; or instead prioritize essentials using existing crews. No broad onboarding or extra retail purchase callback is introduced.

The review shows ready import/export quantities, current treasury, the existing 0.36 per extra handled unit, 300-unit daily ceiling, 108 daily ceiling and 3240 maximum over thirty future days. No budget is reserved and authorization spends nothing immediately. Ordinary handling purchases remain the sole authority. Empty queues cost nothing; river docks, outside voyages, access failures and final delivery are not solved by county crews. Import priority may delay export promises. Extra shifts may be unnecessary; no delivery, vendor sale, attendance or profit is guaranteed.

Only actual import queues at depot -1 qualify. An existing relief authorization, absent qualifying queue or less than 0.36 available treasury disables the paid alternative with an explanation. The review is bound to the town, full current state and RNG; changing cargo, calendar, policy or funds invalidates it. Repeated paid actions cannot extend a current authorization. Priority-only repeat requests already in essentials mode are also rejected without payment. Existing Depot operations controls stop future shifts or change priority; stopping crews alone leaves the chosen priority unchanged. Gathering preparation is not purchased by either logistics alternative and cannot be refunded after its separate purchase.

Validation on the final source: tests/test_market_loading_choice.py and tests/test_civic_calendar.py pass. The first uses the existing labelled controlled paid peak-arrival fixture, not fabricated cargo or cash grants. All cargo is purchased from actual firm balances. Balanced food arrives on day 15; essential priority plus paid shifts arrives on day 13; essential priority with shifts stopped also arrives on day 13. Paid shifts purchase 1113.3216 of actual extra handling across the continuation, but do not improve this food's arrival relative to priority alone. This negative incremental result motivated the no-extra-shift option rather than implying more spending necessarily helps.

A deliberately constrained 0.72-treasury fixture buys only 0.72 of extra handling and leaves nine queued loads. No cash is replenished to claim success. Empty and unfunded eligibility fail; stale and repeated actions fail; existing stop controls prevent future shift spending. Actual gathering-review buttons exercise both logistics choices. Authorizing them does not alter household food/discretionary spending or transfer cash immediately. Daily cash/goods and exact ten-day archive continuation checks pass in the funded comparisons. Existing gathering preparation, participation, cancellation, funding, stale-state and replay tests pass unchanged.

Evidence: docs/evidence/market-loading-choice8, including assembled-source hash. This adds a real budget/priority decision using existing mechanisms; it does not establish general optimal policy, model additional shopper trips or certify Android performance.

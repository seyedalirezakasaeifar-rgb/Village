# Gathering commerce: transaction boundary decision

Inspected combined source 6e8665e plus procurement forecast fix (local c8db9e3). No production economic hook is added.

The existing food flow is authoritative: groceryChoices ranks reachable stores by taxed food price plus actual round-trip travel per needed unit. dailyEconomy buys at most 1.6 food per resident, bounded by household cash after rent, vendor inventory, staffed retail capacity and transit reservations. It transfers money/tax, consumes inventory and records unmet food exactly once. The later discretionary loop computes a separate normal budget after essentials/debt/property reserve, then purchases from existing suppliers.

Gathering participation runs after dailyEconomy through civicCampaignDay. civicGatheringLocalPeople requires h.food>0, adequate health, low stress and no blocked commute/public duty. Thus current attendance is an outcome of today's existing food purchase. Using those participants to run another shopping callback would duplicate consumption or daily budgets. Selecting them earlier from today's food spending is circular; using yesterday's participants is not today's attendance. Giving vendors a price/score preference would bypass the existing landed-cost choice without paying a real detour. Discretionary services currently have no chain-trip cost, venue catchment or individual leisure appointment; bolting that onto gathering completion cannot account for alternative shopping costs truthfully.

A genuine market-day retail extension therefore needs a separate daily trip-planning phase: choose an eligible leisure intention independently of today's realized food spending; compare home→shop→square→home versus home→shop→home under actual walking/transit time, fares and seats; settle the household's single food/discretionary budget once; finalize participation after actual food/access outcomes. Shopping and gathering cancellation must release only unconsumed reservations. New trip intentions/reservations would need canonical save/replay and old-WebView coverage. This is a larger schedule/transport change, not a safe small callback for this deadline. No decorative prosperity multiplier or extra purchases are substituted.

Concrete connected alternative using existing economics: at a market district with waiting essentials cargo, offer the mayor a spending choice between preparing the voluntary gathering and authorizing the existing 30-day extra loading shift. The shift already provides at most 300 extra handling units/day, purchases only work actually needed at 0.36/unit, capped at 108/day, from municipal cash. It can get already-paid food inventories to actual shops sooner; households still buy under their existing need, cash and fare limits. It creates neither household demand nor free cargo. Waiting exports can lose priority; future staffing and goods constraints remain. The gathering retains its honest leisure-only outcome and non-refundable preparation. Do not link shift authorization to automatic attendance or claim extra sales; quote actual queues, costs, arrival risk and food stocks.

Implementation surface for that alternative is a UI comparison of existing authorizeCivicGathering and regionalTrade.reliefUntil controls, with the same explicit separate authorizations. It requires no economic coefficient, event-transaction hook or second purchase budget. UI ownership is separate, so this decision does not mount another control unilaterally.

Required tests before any future retail-trip version: conserved cash/goods and one household budget, empty or closed vendors, unaffordable households, cheaper competing stores, full transit capacity, route interruption, repeated callbacks, before/after cancellation, mid-trip archive replay and canonical RNG. Existing combined-mayor and gathering suites establish funded preparation/attendance only; they cannot establish shopping opportunity costs or sales uplift. Those limits are the reason for declining the small sales hook.

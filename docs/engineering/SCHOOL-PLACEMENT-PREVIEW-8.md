# Public-school placement preview

The construction map now shows a read-only school siting estimate while the cursor hovers a candidate lot. It prices the normal $6,900 build against current treasury and shows the resulting cash, the proposed school's average road distance and residents outside its 40-segment catchment, newly connected residents, and the increase in shared allocatable places out of the school's 260-place standard. That incremental figure uses the same deterministic shared-capacity allocator as public school access, with a virtual facility; it does not add a building or reserve places.

The hint separately shows current education authorization per day, the estimated daily cost of the reachable public standard after this site, the latest actual paid/coverage result when one exists, qualified local unassigned staff, and latest realized staffing when measured. Estimates distinguish geographic reach from operating service, and state that the result does not promise service. The hint wraps to the map width on mobile.

`python tests/test_school_placement_preview.py` checks settlement and regional seed 42 against near and far legal lots, existing-school overlap, a disconnected one-road test site, treasury shortfall, zero eligible local staffing, map rendering and cancellation. Previews leave `G`, its canonical archive and the RNG unchanged; repeated candidate queries are stable. In settlement, the current school already reaches all 169 residents, so a new near or far school adds no theoretical places; the far site's average trip is 30 rather than 14 road segments. In the regional town, each site adds about 260 theoretical places; the far placement leaves 16 residents beyond the 40-segment reach and averages 24 versus 20 segments for the near placement. Its current education authorization is $220/day against an estimated $862/day reachable standard, while 66 qualified local hires are available. The preview correctly does not report funded service before the new school is built and operating.

The disconnected candidate reaches no residents and adds no allocatable places. Reducing treasury to $6,899 marks the $6,900 build unaffordable; removing eligible local staff from the controlled test fixture displays zero local hires. Cancelling the construction tool preserves town state, archive output, cash/goods and RNG. The rendered hint measured about 359 × 101 CSS pixels in a 390 × 844 viewport.

This is a near-term placement estimate based on current households, roads, school capacity, policy and staffing. It does not account for future migration, demand, construction delay or tomorrow's available staff.

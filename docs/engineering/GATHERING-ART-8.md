# Original gathering art

Dependency: calendar API `37d5becb7f8c7d02b9f3c31628e477d32660adc9`, based on `1cf6af5`. Cherry-pick this art commit after that API; regenerate the distribution when combining newer renderer changes. No calendar economics, attendance, schedules or UI controls are changed.

A paid scheduled gathering shows packed covered furniture and a preparation board on the actual square. Active gatherings use two small covered public tables, restrained bunting and two warm lanterns. No displayed goods, new vendors or purchases are invented. Completion, cancellation and failure remove event dressing; the ordinary square remains. Calendar history stays in its existing UI.

Active attendance supplies at most eight representative resident silhouettes (three in fast mode), rather than claiming to track every attendee's trip. Zero attendance produces no event crowd. They enter `buildingActivity()` as ordinary pedestrian ground actors and use the existing actor compositor, including foreground building and elevated-deck occlusion. They are not painted over the completed map. Motion follows the frozen simulation remainder, and distant/reduced-motion/effects/traffic settings gate it before actor creation. Canopies and furniture are cached with the square's original sprite, also providing the correct night emission-occlusion mask; they are not stamped twice by the original painter hook.

The public `paintCivicGathering` hook remains usable in isolation. In normal rendering its static layer is baked into the square, while attendance samples are submitted separately. A bounded cached cue index refreshes during scene preparation and successful event/day transitions. It copies only calendar records and existing active cue descriptors; no routing or household eligibility calculation occurs during painting. Default committed-frame semantics are preserved; experimental cooperative mutation barriers remain the calendar engine's documented integration responsibility.

The separate sprite cache is limited to 32 canvases and 4 MiB total backing storage. Caches use original square-sprite identity plus stage/night, retaining no resident or event objects in their keys. Observed maximum during the viewport tests: 539,392 bytes. Completion requires no new cache entry.

`tests/test_gathering_art.py` uses a paid named Willow Commons event on seed42: 24 actual modelled attendees, at most eight drawn representatives. Twelve cases cover 640×360/DPR1, 844×390/DPR2 and 1280×720/DPR3 with day/night, far detail and reduced motion. Warm/cold paused final frames match exactly, serialized economic state remains unchanged, zero attendance has zero figures, crowd phases differ while static sprite identity stays fixed, and all cache bounds pass. The same-source existing calendar control/accounting/replay suite also passes. Dense adjacent properties and night captures were inspected in `docs/previews/gathering-art/`.

Worst 30-draw warm host mean across these cases was 5.9 ms. This is cached host Chromium painting, not native Android FPS or whole-session latency. The test confirms ordinary ground-actor submission; the existing independent pedestrian/bridge regression supplies depth-policy coverage. No claim is made that these captures include every possible gathering/transit crossing. Native integrated animation review remains pending.

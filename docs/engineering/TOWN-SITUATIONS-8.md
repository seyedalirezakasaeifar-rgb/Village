# Place-based town situations

Base integration `525e47d` plus sequential civic-turnaround commits `e5a590b`, `f83bbbb`. Apply this phase after those commits; it references the real learner-access and operating-office helpers. No economic coefficient or settlement change.

`src/98-town-situations.js` provides three authored situations, selected from current spatial and economic evidence:

* A populated market block containing an actual live retailer: retain existing titles/jobs and inspect access/accounts, or review reserved replacement housing before compensated service-site assembly. Current housing cost and funded duration come from the existing quote; exact clearance remains property-specific.
* A real inbound food corridor competing with a current ready queue or active supplier agreement: review the existing contingent shift, a staffed warehouse investment, or future export restraint. Actual queue units, shop cash and distinct capital/payroll/carriage risks are shown. Cancelling an obligation or paying for a shift still requires its authoritative control.
* A household with eligible learners and less than 60% usable access: bus investment, closer work/teaching, or consolidation before another deed. Actual eligible learners, usable access and local job places ground the situation. The text distinguishes funded teaching, time toward a learner term and eventual qualification; it invents no wage or education penalties.

Route adoption records intent and opens the corresponding existing office. It never performs a purchase. The ordinary controls remain available for other choices; selection is not a mutually exclusive economic lock. Review/cancel and decline are available. There are no automatic progress prizes or duration gates. Up to three places can be followed; the mayor explicitly records dated observations and closes an outcome, including unsuccessful or unchanged conditions. The before/after evidence identifies cash, business continuity/attendance, household home, cargo queues/food, learner access and job places as appropriate. It expressly avoids inferring causation. Historical places retain their coordinates and remain visitable even after the original building is removed.

Optional save data `townSituations` version1 stores a serial, at most three active records, twelve closed records and twenty-four passed keys. Each record pins IDs, world coordinates, route, baseline and at most four dated observations; repeated same-day observation does not duplicate records. `validateTownSituations` is called by archive adoption and rejects invalid references, route names, snapshots and counter bounds. Old saves can omit the field. No daily tick hook or renderer overlay is introduced; the existing agenda project board is wrapped.

Actual-control verification: `tests/test_town_situations.py`. Fresh seed42 selects a real market and underserved household, verifies cancel/no purchase, reaches actual property/transit offices, records the negative result of an unbuilt bus without granting teaching, closes/cancels history correctly, creates a real paid food bridge and signs a supplier agreement to reveal the freight dilemma, and reviews/cancels then authorizes the existing extra shift. Exact replay, malformed archives and optional-data migration are covered. Receipt and screenshot: `reports/town-situations/controls.json`, `reports/town-situations-places.png`.

This phase adds authored context and place memory, not a new causal economy or proof of several-hour enjoyment. Directly repairing every kind of access, successfully assembling/replacing an occupied market, and building a viable bus corridor remain covered by their owning systems' tests rather than claimed as completed by this short journey. Human playtesting and longer comparative town stories remain valuable.

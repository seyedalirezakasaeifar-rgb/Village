# Multi-seed service-access robustness

Six seed/city combinations pair settlement and regional starts at seeds 42,101,909. Each naturally funded private-provider setup begins without public school/health authorization, invites school/fire through ordinary proposals, and allows real construction, hiring, receipts and operating costs. Actual low/middle/high household-spendable quantiles are sampled. Every case archives and reloads at a canonical checkpoint and exact-replays the same next thirty days. Cash/goods ledgers, save validation and living-household accounting are checked.

A matched authorization probe raises education/health ceilings to 1000/day for a real day after the no-authorization sample. Public health forecast increases from 0.646–0.673 to 0.795–0.828 in settlement cities. It stays about 0.434–0.441 in regional cities because accessible facility/staff capacity is limiting. Public school access remains zero in these starts because no reachable public school is available; public/private school *funding* does not manufacture a nearby place. The forecasts do not count nominal budget as service where access is absent.

Payroll stress drains the actual private school firm's cash into the finite regional account and records zero provider working cash; no replacement funds enter the provider. Admission forecasts continue to reject that school's unfunded payroll capacity. Synthetic road disconnection removes only actual adjacent road tiles for the access observation, then restores the same tiles. The disconnected household's public-service forecast drops to zero or is reduced by reach, and returns to its previous value on reconnection. These access/payroll checks are controlled transient perturbations, not permanent disaster or all-city transit studies.

Across these six focused runs, cash drift stays within 0.00000012, goods errors stay under0.001 and every archive replay is byte-identical. No inconsistency is reproduced. Existing doctors/schools forecasts use accessible facilities, legal authorized budgets, and persisted or ready staffing; private-school forecasts require live staff, spare service capacity and enough actual provider and household cash for payroll/fees. Realized purchases stay at the wallets and fees observed that day. A forecast can be lower than a later funded outcome because it is not a reservation.

Limitations: seed42/101/909 are a small deterministic sample; the regional founding mix has high overall town liquidity, so its lower quantile still exceeds settlement lower quantiles. The generated starts in this test do not contain a reachable public school, so it cannot establish the benefit of full school siting. It demonstrates that neither migration assessment nor public-health forecast treats a distant, unstaffed or unaffordable facility as usable access. This is QA evidence, not calibrated real-world income classes or a long-term outcome claim.

Reproduce: `python tests/test_service_access_households.py` and `python tests/test_service_access_funding_matrix.py`. Source hashes and full household rows are retained under docs/evidence/service-affordability-matrix8.

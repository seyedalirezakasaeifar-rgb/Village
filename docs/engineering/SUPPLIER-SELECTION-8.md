# Discoverable buyer, commodity and supplier alternatives

Base: `03cb546` plus public-life `f9b968d`. This commit is separate from public-life and changes UI selection/review guards only. No reserve, production, price, dispatch, payroll or settlement coefficient is changed.

`alternativeSupplierPanel()` adds one collapsed comparison disclosure after the three preferred supplier cards. It starts at Greyhaven food and offers buyer, buyer-requested commodity and actual open producer selects. A buyer with no matching producer remains visible with an explanation; it is not represented as absent demand. Closed businesses are excluded from supplier selection. Preferences are transient UI data, reset with town state, not an archive schema addition.

The comparison uses existing `regionalContractForecast` and displays held/retained units, already promised units, estimated/suggested commitment, enterprise freight cash, quoted unit price/freight, suggested-size inventory margin, recent output, dispatch days and deadline. Reasons distinguish missing road, no dispatch days, cash shortage and fewer than ten forecast units. The ordinary risky full-window review remains available through the existing handler; it is explicitly risky and does not bypass retained stock or dispatch policy. Three active agreements disable another review.

Buttons use the same `data-trade-offer/firm/good` attributes as preferred cards. The common reviewed authorization now validates supplier output and market eligibility, captures the current town/day and a forecast stamp, and rejects a stale quote before creating any obligation. The stamp includes actual cash, stock, price, freight, travel days, reserve, existing promises and current output/suggestion. Cancellation remains the existing control; pending cargo retains existing terms. No new economic transaction path exists.

Actual-control evidence: `tests/test_supplier_selection.py`, `reports/supplier-selection/controls.json`, `food-comparison.png`. Fresh seed42 initially shows food held80 versus reserve104, suggested0; after25 ordinary settlement days the same supplier holds496.08 versus reserve112.31 and suggests200, with forecast margin416 before payroll/tax/disruption. The player selects food, cancels, rejects a captured stale-town authorization, then signs a real forecast-sized200-unit food agreement.35 further ordinary days fulfil that agreement through real dispatch and paid delivery. No cash, stock, staffing or production fixture grants are used. An Upland commodity list correctly excludes food because that buyer does not request it. This is one deterministic feasibility comparison, not a guarantee that every town will profit or fulfil future agreements.

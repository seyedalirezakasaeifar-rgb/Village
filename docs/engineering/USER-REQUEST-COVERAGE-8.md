## Realistic final integration order

1. **Now:** retain 01eeda as the authoritative starting model and finish the native owner's exact parser/provider/lifecycle rerun. Keep source/hash manifests and ordinary controls as the parity oracle.
2. **Critical path, in parallel:** graphics owner produces measured usable normal quality plus actual selected-style visual/animation acceptance. Worker owner rebases and completes current control ownership/durability. Renderer progress must not assume that an incomplete Worker fixes GPU cost. If the visual/runtime target is still not credible around 09:00 UTC, escalate the delivery risk early rather than relabel a diagnostic or slow tier as finished.
3. **Compose only complete candidates:** preserve every production control, settings, audio lifecycle, creator guard and save fence. No old whole-tree replacement. Review any global admission candidate separately; record its actual safe envelope rather than promise unlimited cities.
4. **Target a coherent accepted feature candidate by about 11:30 UTC:** ordinary-resource expansion/service/trade/transit/migration journeys; complete-state/RNG/archive faults; native export/import/cancel/Back/process death; real camera/depth/LOD/edge tests; representative city sizes, frame/input/heap budgets and human visual/audio review. Resolve defects before 13:00 UTC; that is a completion deadline, not permission to leave intended features unfinished behind a freeze.
5. **13:00–14:00 UTC:** coordinated measured optimization, retest every applied optimization on the exact assembled source, then final packaging. Confirm intended signer/certificate and upgrade compatibility; do not regenerate a key or uninstall a user's town merely to obtain a passing install.
6. **Deliver:** verify signature, alignment, package/version and full APK/embedded-asset hashes against the accepted commit; install/launch/upgrade smoke test the intended artifact; retain backup/recovery instructions and disclose measured runtime limits. Do not deliver an old QA package or a stale APK link. Parent owns signing, publication and final handoff.

The highest-value remaining playable work is usable selected-style graphics, preservation of the complete existing control surface in any Worker composition, and exact final native/delivery acceptance. Additional broad feature systems would increase risk without closing those gaps. Deadline anchors remain 13:00/14:00 UTC (BC permanent UTC−07); no Nov1 fallback was applied.

# Original brownfield site kit

Dependency: `125f9b0e6ddedda7fd22ceffa2a4eb1ecdf5ff38` brownfield engine. Cherry-pick the art commit only after that API is integrated. Runtime ownership is the new `98zzzzzzzzz-brownfield-art.js`, plus regenerated `assets/game.js`; no economic algorithms or shared stylesheet changes.

Assessment adds a modest survey board alongside the retained industrial premises. Acquired land gains a secured, muted earth treatment. Only a paid remediating site receives material piles and parked original ground-preparation equipment; its board uses actual remaining workdays. Nothing moves or advances from render time, including disconnected/unfunded work. Cleared land has a level graded surface and green reuse marker. Actual new housing, shop and park construction use their existing architectural kit, with a small site-history marker. No benefit, production or pollution recovery is invented.

Descriptors are copied into static scene items during prepareScene; drawing reads those copies. Successful project actions and brownfieldDay invalidate the scene only when descriptors change. Existing save-load/static preparation handles town replacement. Reserved footprints suppress procedural trees/street furniture on their own legal tiles, and fence edges omit internal tile boundaries. Site sprites participate in ordinary depth repair and night emission masking. Zoom below .4 drops the fence, equipment and marker details, retaining just the land treatment. No per-frame site/household/route scan is added.

A separate FIFO sprite cache is capped at 32 sprites of 72×96 pixels, at most 884,736 backing bytes. Cache keys use visual stage/progress/edge/detail only, never project ID. Per-site scene items follow the authoritative footprint; no full-world texture is created.

`tests/test_brownfield_art.py` follows a controlled quarry closure through actual paid assessment/acquisition/ground works and all three funded reuse arms. Captures in `docs/previews/brownfield-art/` show every stage and night. Repeated paused draws preserve both exact pixels and serialized economic state; distant rendering and cache bounds pass. This fixture is a one-tile quarry, not proof of every multi-tile city configuration. The existing full brownfield renewal control/accounting/archive suite also passes. Host cached-frame timings and source hashes are in the receipt; these are not Android FPS claims.

Civic gathering art is not added: no gathering descriptor/API was supplied with this task. Native acceptance and integrated cooperative-snapshot review remain with integration.

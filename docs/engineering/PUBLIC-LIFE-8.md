Base: combined stable `03cb54675880a9b60c0c8d8de5dd0b671d0abff3`. This slice changes player-facing planning and optional place records, not economic coefficients or redevelopment algorithms.

Quotes show surrounding residents, retailers and attending workers; the full actual footprint; separate construction prices; and the opportunity cost of using the site for public space versus staffed freight premises. ## Food-offer investigation for economy lead

No reserve policy or contract selection algorithm was changed. UI evidence points to supplier selection before feasibility filtering: `districtPreferredSupplier(m,index)` in `src/24-district-programmes.js` returns one producer per market, sorting district contract size first, then the index-selected preferred good, then held stock. The preferences selected by current export-key order are Upland timber, Greyhaven flour, Ironridge manufactures. Food buyers exist at Greyhaven and Ironridge, but food firms usually never reach the displayed slot. `regionalTradePanel()` shows only that single result for each market. Therefore zero visible food offers do not establish zero feasible food production or a reserve-policy defect.

Recommended coordinated UI correction: disclose eligible alternative producers/goods with their existing `regionalContractForecast`, including the exact retained food reserve and zero/positive suggested quantity; route selection through the existing reviewed contract handler. Do not lower reserves or turn every food producer into a guaranteed profitable offer. This remains with the integration/economy lead to avoid conflicting selection changes.

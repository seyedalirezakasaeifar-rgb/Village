THE TOWN CHARTER

You run the local council, not an all-powerful state. Residents earn wages and pay taxes. Farms supply mills, mills supply bakeries, bakeries supply grocers. Businesses invest or fail, and families make ends meet — or don't.

  1. Start with the Agenda. Pick an ambition to pursue and see how council trust is changing.
  2. Choose policies deliberately. Adjust taxes, social programmes, security and trade, then enact a package of changes.
  3. Let time pass. Firms hire, trade and invest. Track changes for individual families in the People tab.
  4. Use optional public works. Private development operates without your input. Roads must connect. Real deliveries and commuters depend on access, travel distance and maintenance. Tap a road with the resurfacing tool to repair it.
  5. Check your inbox when ready. Council correspondence is recorded without pausing the simulation, even at 64×. Respond whenever you choose.
  6. Survive elections. Every 360 days, residents judge your record. You can continue even after losing, in sandbox mode.

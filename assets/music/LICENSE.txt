TownLedger original chamber soundtrack — CC0-1.0

All ten pieces listed in score.json contain original melodies, arrangements
and additive synthesis authored for this project in tools/compose_music.py
and tools/compose_extended_score.py. The seven longer arrangements include
contrasting sections, original responses, bridges, codas and instrumentation.
No external samples, recordings or borrowed melodies were used.
To the extent permitted by law, the project dedicates these compositions,
recordings and score generation code to the public domain under CC0 1.0:

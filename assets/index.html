THE TOWN CHARTER

You run the local council, not an all-powerful state. Residents earn wages and pay taxes. Farms supply mills, mills supply bakeries, bakeries supply grocers. Businesses invest or fail, and families make ends meet — or don't.

  1. Start with the Agenda. Pick an ambition to pursue and see how council trust is changing.
  2. Diagnose local services. In Services, compare funded coverage with reachable places, staff and operating funds. The access map and household inspector show road catchments. Regional recruitment takes 30 days; adult training needs 120 funded teaching days. The budget shortcut opens the relevant staged policy.
  3. Choose policies deliberately. Adjust taxes, social programmes, security and trade, then enact a package of changes.
  4. Observe the result. Change a small package, let several weeks pass, then compare the Policy journal with business sales and family accounts. Firms need customers and materials before cheaper credit can create jobs. Firms hire, trade and invest. Track changes for individual families in the People tab.
  5. Follow cash and credit. Families meet essentials and scheduled loan payments before optional purchases. Banks compare stressed repayments with income and reserves. Firms order capacity only when demand and margins justify it; funded equipment takes 30 days to commission. Inspect a borrower for its latest credit decision.
  6. Use optional public works. Private development operates without your input. Roads must connect. Real deliveries and commuters depend on access, travel distance and maintenance. Tap a road with the resurfacing tool to repair it.
  7. Expand with a deed. Open Public Works → Expand the borough to preview twenty-one substantial outer districts. Confirm the treasury price to buy new development rights. Roads, utilities, accessible services and private investment still have to reach the new neighbourhood; existing private titles remain intact.
  8. Work the water. In Public Works → Waterfront, site fisheries beside water and a connected road. Crews land real food within a shared catch limit. Cargo wharves need a river outlet, workers and competitively priced export orders. The eastern lake supports fishing, but has no regional shipping outlet.
  9. Check your inbox when ready. Council correspondence is recorded without pausing the simulation, even at 64×. Respond whenever you choose.
  10. Survive elections. Every 360 days, residents judge your record. You can continue even after losing, in sandbox mode.
